Margin call when equity falls below maintenance (here 25% × MV). Leverage magnifies losses as well as gains.

Tutor. Deposit cash, buy more stock than cash alone would allow. Reg T initial margin is 50%: on a $10,000 purchase put up at least $5,000; the rest is a debit loan. Drag mark-to-market down and watch equity shrink. When equity % hits the maintenance floor, you are in a margin call.

Tutor. Sell short at spot — borrow and sell. Profit if price falls; loss if price rises — no ceiling on upside, so risk is theoretically unlimited. Play the path, then Buy to cover. Series 65: shorts require a margin account; the short seller may owe dividends to the lender.
