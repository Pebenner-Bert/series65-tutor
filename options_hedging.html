OPTIONS HEDGING → THE ONLY 4 ANSWERS

Series 65 · every stock + option question reduces to one of these four boxes
MEMORIZE THESE TWO LINES — EVERYTHING ELSE IS DERIVED
LONG stock→ PUT protects·CALL pays
SHORT stock→ CALL protects·PUT pays
BUY the option — PROTECTyou PAY premium · caps your loss
SELL the option — INCOMEyou GET premium · that's your max gain
LONG
stockyou own it
risk = DROP
BUY A PUT
= PROTECTIVE PUT (insurance)
Put gains as the stock falls, offsetting the loss. Upside stays unlimited. Cue words: “protect,” “hedge,” “limit downside,” “insurance.”
SELL A CALL
= COVERED CALL (income)
Premium is the income; “covered” because you hold the shares. Cost: capped above the strike. Cue words: “income,” “profit from a flat/mild market.”
SHORT
stockyou borrowed+sold
risk = RISE
BUY A CALL
= caps a short's unlimited risk
Short stock loses as price rises — with no ceiling. The call locks a buy-back price. Cue words: “short 100sh … protect against risk.”
SELL A PUT
= income, NOT protection
Collects premium but adds risk if the stock falls. If a question says “protect” and you are short, this is a distractor.
WHY “protect” = BUY. Buying gives you a right, and a buyer’s max loss is only the premium. Insurance costs money and caps damage — that is protection.
WHY “income” = SELL. Selling collects the premium but takes on an obligation. Max gain = premium received. Cash now, risk later — that is income, not a hedge.

BREAKEVEN — “call up, put down”:  CALL BE = strike + premium  ·  PUT BE = strike − premium.
Buy 50 call @ 3 → BE 53, max loss 3, gain unlimited.  Buy 50 put @ 2 → BE 48, max loss 2, max gain 48.
IN-the-money: call = stock above strike · put = stock below strike.  Premium = intrinsic + time.  Expires 3rd Friday. Options/futures = derivatives.

⚠️ THE HALF-RULE THAT WILL COST YOU Q11

“Long stock → buy put, short stock → buy call” is only the PROTECT column. Q11 says long stock and asks how to PROFIT — the half-rule answers “buy a put,” but the key says SELL A CALL. So always read for the verb first: protect / hedge / limit → BUY.  income / profit / generate → SELL. Position picks the type; the verb picks buy or sell.
